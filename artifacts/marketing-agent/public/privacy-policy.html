Last updated: May 25, 2026

Introduction

GrowIQ ("we", "us", or "our") operates the GrowIQ AI Marketing Agent available at growiqai.replit.app.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our service. By using GrowIQ, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: First name, last name, and email address when you register
  • Usage data: How you interact with our platform, campaigns you create, and features you use
  • Social media tokens: Access tokens for Facebook and Instagram (used only to publish content on your behalf)
  • Campaign data: Content, images, and text you create through our platform

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the GrowIQ service
  • Publish content to your social media accounts on your behalf
  • Create and manage advertising campaigns you request
  • Send you important service notifications
  • Improve our AI marketing features
  • Respond to your support requests

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): To publish content and ads on your behalf via official APIs
  • Google Ads: To create and manage campaigns on your behalf via official APIs
  • OpenAI: To generate marketing content and images (no personal data shared)
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Access tokens are stored securely and never shared
  • We regularly review and update our security practices
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

Cookies

GrowIQ uses essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.
